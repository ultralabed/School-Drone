How do you play School Drone?

You press big buttons to fly a little drone around a library. A robot teacher inside the drone tells you what each button does.

How do you start playing?

Open the game and press Up. You do not need to install anything or sign in.

What does each button do?

  1. Take off: Press Up. The propellers spin fast and the drone lifts off the yellow landing pad.
  2. Fly around: Press Up, Down, Left or Right. Hold a button to keep going that way.
  3. Stop in the air: Press Hover. The drone stays still in the air.
  4. Point where to go: Tap any place in the library picture. The drone flies to that spot.
  5. Do the bee dance: Press the 8 button. The drone flies a figure 8 two times and the kids cheer.
  6. Watch the battery: Look at the 3 batteries. When the flying battery gets low, the teacher tells you to land.
  7. Land: Press Land. The drone flies back to the yellow pad and comes down slowly.
  8. Pick a drone: Choose 4 propellers, helicopter, jet or 6 propellers. The teacher explains how each one flies.

Which keys work on a keyboard?

The arrow keys fly the drone, the space bar is Hover, the 8 key is the bee dance and the L key is Land.

How do you change the language?

Press the 中文 or English button at the top of the game. The buttons and the teacher change language right away.

How do you turn the teacher's voice on or off?

Press Voice: on next to the teacher's words. The words always show on the screen, even when the voice is off.

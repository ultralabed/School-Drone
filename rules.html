What are the rules of School Drone?

There are three big rules: take turns, land before the battery is empty, and keep the drone away from people and walls.

Who gets to fly?

One kid flies with one battery. When the battery is empty, a fresh battery goes in and the next kid becomes the pilot. The pilot's name shows at the top of the game.

How do the 3 batteries work?

One battery is in the drone and the other two charge. A full battery flies for about 35 seconds, and an empty one takes about 50 seconds to fill up again.

  • The battery only goes down while the drone is in the air.
  • When the battery is low, the teacher says it is time to land.
  • When the battery is empty, the drone flies back to the pad by itself.

How high and how low can the drone fly?

The drone can fly up to 3.2 meters high, just under the ceiling. It will not go lower than 0.3 meters until you press Land.

Where is the drone not allowed to go?

The drone stops before the walls on the left and right. The teacher reminds you to keep away from walls and bookshelves.

Where does the drone land?

The drone always lands on the yellow pad in the middle of the blue rug. Press Land and it flies there by itself.

What are good safety rules for a real drone?

A real drone needs more care than a game drone. These are good habits for kids:

  • Fly with a grown-up.
  • Keep the drone away from faces, people and pets.
  • Never touch the propellers while they spin.
  • Fly where there is lots of open space.
  • Always keep the drone where you can see it.
  • Land before the battery is empty.

Rules for real drones are different in each country. Ask a grown-up to check the rules where you live.

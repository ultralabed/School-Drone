School Drone questions and answers

School Drone is a free game that teaches kids how drones fly. Here are short answers to common questions.

What is School Drone?

School Drone is a free web game where kids fly a drone inside a library. A robot teacher inside the drone explains each move in simple words.

Is School Drone free?

Yes, School Drone is free. There are no ads, no sign-in and nothing to buy.

Do I need to install anything?

No. School Drone runs in a web browser. Open the page and press Up.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The buttons are big enough to tap, and you can also tap the library picture to send the drone there.

What do kids learn?

Kids learn how a drone goes up, down, left and right, what hover means, why pilots watch the battery, and how to keep a drone away from people and walls.

Why does the drone have 3 batteries?

Three batteries let every kid have a turn. One battery flies while the other two charge, so there is always a fresh one ready.

Why did the drone land by itself?

The drone lands by itself when its battery is empty. A fresh battery goes in and the next kid becomes the pilot.

What does hover mean?

Hover means the drone stays still in the air. The propellers push just hard enough to hold it up.

Why is the figure 8 called the bee dance?

Honeybees dance in a figure 8 to show other bees where flowers are. The drone copies that shape.

Which drones can I pick?

You can pick a drone with 4 propellers, a helicopter, a jet, or a drone with 6 propellers. The teacher explains how each one flies.

Which languages does the game have?

The game has English and Traditional Chinese. Press the 中文 or English button at the top to switch.

Why can't I hear the teacher?

The voice uses the speech built into your device, and some devices do not have one. Check that Voice is on and the sound is up. The teacher's words always show on the screen.

Who made School Drone?

The idea for School Drone came from Aaron Shih, and it was built by his dad, Edward Shih, as a kids' project. You can find Edward on LinkedIn.

Can teachers and parents use it or change it?

Yes. School Drone is open source under the MIT license, so you may use it, copy it and change it. Please keep the author's name. The code is on GitHub.
